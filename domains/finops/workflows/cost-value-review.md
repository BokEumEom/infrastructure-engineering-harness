# Cost and Value Review

1. Resolve the FinOps scope, owner, allocation dimensions, and business unit metric.
2. Gather current cost, usage, utilization, rate, and business-volume evidence.
3. Compare total cost and unit cost; never infer waste from total spend growth alone.
4. Check reliability, performance, security, contractual and migration constraints before recommending optimization.
5. Separate usage optimization, rate/commitment optimization, allocation correction, and architecture/workload-placement decisions.
6. Quantify expected savings or cost avoidance when evidence permits, and state uncertainty.
7. For commitment decisions, verify demand stability and approval policy.

Required output: cost/value state, evidence, allocation quality, unit economics, opportunity type, expected value, risk/constraints, verification, and recommendation.
