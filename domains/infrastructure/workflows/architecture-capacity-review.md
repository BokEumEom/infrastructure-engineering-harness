# Architecture and Capacity Review

1. Resolve the affected service and dependency graph.
2. State the problem and current constraint in provider-neutral terms.
3. Gather evidence for utilization, saturation, latency, errors, capacity, and recent changes when applicable.
4. Compare the proposal with ADRs and incident history.
5. Identify failure-mode, blast-radius, security, durability, operability, and cost trade-offs.
6. Prefer the smallest reversible experiment when evidence is incomplete.
7. Produce a change proposal only when the decision is supported.

Required output: decision, evidence, assumptions, trade-offs, verification, migration/rollback requirements, ADR impact, and cross-domain handoffs to SRE/DevOps/FinOps when relevant.
