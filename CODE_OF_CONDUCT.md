# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful to infrastructure, SRE, DevOps, FinOps, Security and agent-engineering practitioners with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific about evidence, assumptions and trade-offs.
- Critique ideas, designs and results rather than people.
- Make room for contributors who can bring operational experience even if they are not framework maintainers.
- Treat disagreement across reliability, security, cost and delivery as useful engineering information rather than something to suppress.
- Do not pressure contributors to disclose employer, customer or production details.
- Respect responsible disclosure for security-sensitive findings.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, credential sharing, publishing private production data, or deliberately encouraging unsafe infrastructure changes are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

When reporting conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub rather than escalating sensitive personal details in a public issue.
